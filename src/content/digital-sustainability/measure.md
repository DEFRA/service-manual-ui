## Act before you can measure

You do not need a metric to take action. Many of the most useful actions need no measurement first, such as:

- collecting and storing less data
- turning off environments when you are not using them
- reusing components rather than building them from scratch
- keeping hardware in use for as long as possible, and reusing or recycling it at the end

## Record what you measure

Record your figures, and how you got them, in your [digital sustainability statement](/digital-sustainability/service-standard/process). Use the [sustainability questions for alpha and beta](/digital-sustainability/service-standard/questions) to check your approach at each phase.

## Why we measure environmental and social impacts

Measuring environmental and social impacts helps teams:

- identify where action is needed
- prioritise improvements
- make evidence-based decisions
- track progress over time
- check whether an improvement has worked

<div class="govuk-inset-text">

Measure to make better decisions about your service. A number is not the goal.

</div>

The impacts you can measure depend on the service, the technology and the lifecycle stage you are assessing.

These impacts happen during:

- raw material extraction and processing
- manufacturing
- transport
- operation
- reuse, recycling and disposal

Knowing the impacts of your service across its lifecycle shows you how action on sustainability can contribute to Defra's 6 objectives.

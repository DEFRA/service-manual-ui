**Applies to:** Defra Commercial, procurement teams, and service or project teams that write requirements for something being bought. That could be hosting, software, AI, hardware or a delivery partner.

Procurement often happens at departmental level, through Defra Commercial, so it may be outside a service or project team's control. Project teams can still influence some procurements. Some take place once delivery has begun.

If your project will need procurement, say so in your [digital sustainability risk assessment](/digital-sustainability/service-standard/process). If an earlier procurement limits what your service can do, record it in your digital sustainability statement.

## Procurement frameworks and guidance in UK government

- [Government Commercial Agency](https://www.gca.gov.uk/) (GCA): guidance, commercial agreements, supplier information and procurement tools to help UK public sector organisations buy goods and services
- [Procurement Act 2023 guidance documents](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents) (Cabinet Office): technical guidance on applying the Act and its regulations, from planning to contract management

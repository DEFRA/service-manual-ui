## Procurement frameworks and guidance in UK Government

There are a number of frameworks and sources of guidance for conducting procurement in UK Government. These include:

- The [Government Commercial Agency (GCA)](https://www.gca.gov.uk/) website provides guidance, commercial agreements, supplier information and procurement tools to help UK public sector organisations buy goods and services effectively and achieve value for the nation
- The [Procurement Act 2023 guidance](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/procurement-act-2023-guidance-documents/) documents provide technical guidance for public procurement practitioners on interpreting and applying the Procurement Act 2023 and associated regulations across the planning, defining, procuring and contract management stages of the commercial lifecycle

## (i) Align with relevant governmental strategies and commitments on sustainability, along with any commitments which update and extend them:

Suppliers should comply with the following key sustainability strategies in UK Government:

- The [ICT and Digital Services Strategy (2020 to 2025),](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/greening-government-ict-and-digital-services-strategy-2020-2025/) sets out how government, suppliers and industry should work together to provide responsible and resilient ICT and digital services that support environmental goals, including net zero and the UN Sustainable Development Goals
- The [Greening Government Commitments (2021 to 2025),](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/greening-government-commitments-2021-to-2025/greening-government-commitments-2021-to-2025/) set out how UK government organisations should reduce and report the environmental impacts of their operations, including their use of ICT and digital services

---

## (ii) Follow the appropriate Government Buying Standards

The UK's [Government Buying Standards (GBS)](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/sustainable-procurement-the-government-buying-standards-gbs/) set mandatory minimum sustainability criteria for central government procurement of goods and services.

The anticipated update to the Government Buying Standards will set out more requirements across 4 categories of digital technology, covering:

- Software
- Artificial Intelligence
- Hosting
- Hardware

Where [Government Buying Standards](https://www.gov.uk/government/collections/sustainable-procurement-the-government-buying-standards-gbs) do not exist for a particular technology category, suppliers are encouraged to comply with recognised sustainability standards and frameworks, such as [Green Public Procurement criteria](https://green-forum.ec.europa.eu/green-business/green-public-procurement/gpp-criteria-and-requirements_en), [ENERGY STAR](https://www.energystar.gov/products/computers), [TCO Certified](https://tcocertified.com/), [EPEAT](https://www.epeat.net/) or equivalent schemes.

---

## (iii) Follow frameworks informing digital project assurance

When designing, implementing and delivering digital services and other projects, suppliers should adopt the applicable elements of the following guidance and standards:

- The [Technology Code of Practice](https://www.gov.uk/guidance/the-technology-code-of-practice/) is a set of criteria to help government design, build and buy technology. Point 12 of the Tech Code of Practice is focused on sustainable technology.
- The [GOV.UK Service Manual](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/environmentally-sustainable-services/) page on a 'Environmentally sustainable services' recommends measuring and reducing environmental impacts throughout the service lifecycle, including those associated with software, hardware, data, infrastructure, suppliers and users.

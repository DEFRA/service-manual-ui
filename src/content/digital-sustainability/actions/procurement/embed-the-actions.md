## (i) Embed the Digital Sustainability Actions when designing, developing and delivering services

As best practice, suppliers are encourage to adopt the Digital Sustainability Actions when designing, implementing and delivery government services.

This could involve:

- Identifying which of the DSAs are revelant and actionable for a given project
- Integrating the DSAs into non-functional requirements, KPIs and contract clauses

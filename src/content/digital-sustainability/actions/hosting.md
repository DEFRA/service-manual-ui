Data centres can be divided into the following three broad categories:

- On-premises data centres – infrastructure owned or controlled by an organisation and hosted within its own premises, giving greater direct control over hardware, energy use, cooling and operations.
- Off-premises data centres – infrastructure hosted in an external facility, such as a colocation data centre, where space, power and cooling are provided by a third party while the organisation may retain control of its hardware.
- Cloud data centres – computing, storage and other infrastructure provided on demand by cloud providers, with physical infrastructure shared between customers and resources able to scale according to demand.

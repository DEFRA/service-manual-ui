**Applies to:** every kind of digital work, from discovery onwards. That includes services on and off the Core Delivery Platform (CDP), internal tools, infrastructure projects and procurement.

Most teams start here. These actions cover what to find out in discovery, what to put in the business case and plan, and how to measure progress.

Services also need to [assess sustainability risks and record their actions](/digital-sustainability/service-standard/process) to meet [Defra's 15th point on sustainability](/digital-sustainability/service-standard). Plan this alongside your other [governance and assurance](/product-and-delivery/governance) steps.

The actions in this theme are:

- [1a. Factor sustainability into project research](/digital-sustainability/actions/research-and-planning/research): find out which goals apply and where the environmental impacts are
- [1b. Include sustainability in business cases, requirements and planning](/digital-sustainability/actions/research-and-planning/business-cases-and-planning): put impact, whole-life cost and climate risk into the business case, requirements and plan
- [1c. Measure and report on sustainability](/digital-sustainability/actions/research-and-planning/measure-and-report): model your options, choose metrics, set a baseline and share your approach

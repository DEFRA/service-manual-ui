For projects in Defra, these actions provide guidance on how teams can deliver on the objectives of the Defra [Digital Sustainability Strategy 2025 to 2030.](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/defra-digital-sustainability-strategy-2025-to-2030/defra-digital-sustainability-strategy-2025-to-2030/) For other departments and public sector bodies they represent advice on sustainability best practice.

The Digital Sustainability Actions have been developed through an open and collaborative process involving colleagues from a wide range of disciplines across UK government and the [Government Digital Sustainability Alliance](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/government-digital-sustainability-alliance-gdsa/).

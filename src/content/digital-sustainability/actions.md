## About the actions

For projects in Defra, the actions show how teams can meet the objectives of the [Defra digital sustainability strategy 2025 to 2030](https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/defra-digital-sustainability-strategy-2025-to-2030/defra-digital-sustainability-strategy-2025-to-2030) (GOV.UK). For other departments and public sector bodies, they are advice on good sustainability practice.

The actions were developed openly with colleagues from a wide range of disciplines across UK government and the [Government Digital Sustainability Alliance](https://www.gov.uk/government/groups/government-digital-sustainability-alliance-gdsa) (GOV.UK).

<h2 class="govuk-heading-m">How we choose</h2>

<p class="govuk-body">A panel scores every request against 8 criteria. The criteria answer 4 questions:</p>

<ul class="govuk-list govuk-list--bullet">
  <li>Is the problem worth solving?</li>
  <li>Is it ready to build on?</li>
  <li>Is AI the right answer?</li>
  <li>Will it help anyone else?</li>
</ul>

<p class="govuk-body">Most requests stop here.</p>

<p class="govuk-body"><a class="govuk-link" href="/ai-toolkit/projects/how-we-score">Read how we score a request</a>.</p>

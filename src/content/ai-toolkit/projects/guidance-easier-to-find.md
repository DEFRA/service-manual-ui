<h2 class="govuk-heading-m">The problem</h2>

<p class="govuk-body">We started by building a check that reviews a document before it is published. It works.</p>

<p class="govuk-body">But a check only makes one document better. It does nothing for how fast people find guidance, whether they trust it, or how it stays up to date.</p>

<h2 class="govuk-heading-m">What we are building</h2>

<p class="govuk-body">We are taking one area of guidance and proving it end to end. That means:</p>

<ul class="govuk-list govuk-list--bullet">
  <li>fast search, so people find the right answer quickly</li>
  <li>content that gives the answer first, then the background</li>
  <li>version control, a named owner and a full audit trail</li>
  <li>an agreed quality check before anything is published</li>
</ul>

<p class="govuk-body">One area done properly tells us whether the idea works, and we scale up only if the numbers back it.</p>

<h2 class="govuk-heading-m">What it does not cover</h2>

<p class="govuk-body">How guidance gets written stays in today's process. The casework itself is untouched.</p>

<h2 class="govuk-heading-m">Where it has got to</h2>

<p class="govuk-body">We are sizing the problem in numbers and agreeing how to measure it.</p>

<p class="govuk-body">Next we build and test with case workers, over and over. Then one area goes live in test. After that we review and decide whether to widen it.</p>

<p class="govuk-body">Each round runs the same way: build, test with people, refine. The rounds overlap.</p>

<h2 class="govuk-heading-m">How we will know it works</h2>

<p class="govuk-body">We measure the same things before and after, so we can see what changed. We want guidance that helps people:</p>

<ul class="govuk-list govuk-list--bullet">
  <li>take the right action first time, measured by how many quality checks pass</li>
  <li>trust what they read, measured by whether people use the latest version</li>
  <li>work faster, measured by the time it takes to find an answer</li>
  <li>ask fewer questions, measured by queries passed to managers and experts</li>
</ul>

<p class="govuk-body">A guidance designer at the Rural Payments Agency is taking the baseline numbers now. We agree the measures together before the build starts.</p>

<h2 class="govuk-heading-m">What comes later</h2>

<p class="govuk-body">We are proving one area first. These wait until it works:</p>

<ul class="govuk-list govuk-list--bullet">
  <li>a search assistant across all guidance</li>
  <li>AI writing guidance as well as checking it</li>
  <li>rolling out to more areas</li>
  <li>handling whole cases automatically</li>
</ul>
